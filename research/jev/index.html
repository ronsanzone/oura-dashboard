UPDATED CONTEXT VOCABULARY

Useful context.
Not a questionnaire about everything.

General topics: work demands, evening work, travel/time-zone changes, routine disruption, caffeine, meal timing, exercise context, recovery activities, sleep circumstances, and social context. Medication topics: Adderall and Zepbound, separately opt-in. Capture taken / explicitly not taken / unknown / conflicting, stated time, stated dose + unit, and explicitly reported changes. Medication text needs explicit external-processing consent and an outgoing-excerpt preview before inference. Adding these topics to the plan does not authorize transmitting real notes. Keep source text and corrections editable, and delete derived records with their source when requested.

Pilot the interview separately from the classifier.

Compare a fixed short check-in with adaptive routing using the same approved question bank and a frozen chronological holdout. Measure unsupported-label errors, correction rate, useful-answer yield, unknown coverage, skip burden, and completion time. Test missing medication mentions, caffeine-free versus whole-day absence, airport-without-travel, retrospective answers, conflicting answers, disabled topics, repeats, Done, and the hard question cap. Target roughly one minute and no more than two follow-ups; do not reward more questions or fewer unknowns at the expense of correctness. All outcomes remain hidden from routing and classification.

First evaluate synthetic interviews offline. Then, with separate consent, include Daily Debrief in the 14-day usability pilot. Multi-turn routing adds calls and tokens: meter them under the same hard budget, with a manual fallback if the model is unavailable or the budget is exhausted. 05 / EVIDENCE STANDARD

Three kinds of uncertainty.
Never one “confidence” badge.

A

Classifier certainty

How concentrated is Jev’s answer distribution? Choice and Score return confidence; Noul returns a yes probability, not a separate confidence field.

“The note probably means travel.”
B

Statistical uncertainty

How precisely does this history estimate a difference? Intervals depend on sample size, serial dependence, missingness, and the chosen comparison.

“This difference is imprecise.”
C

Causal uncertainty

Could travel, workload, reporting bias, or another factor explain both? Neither model certainty nor a narrow interval removes confounding.

“We do not know what caused it.”
01

Normalize first; preserve missingness.

Keep Oura’s supplied day for daily aggregates; do not reinterpret a date-only field through UTC. Resolve timestamped notes in America/New_York, preserving original offsets and travel ambiguity. Join by local day, never array position. Validate units and sync completeness. Missing stress stays missing, never zero. “No note” stays unknown. Keep a per-day eligibility ledger; an aggregate alone does not guarantee full wear coverage.

02

Detect change without learning a story.

Proposed alert rule: compare a trailing 7-day median (at least 5 valid days) with the preceding 28-day median (at least 21 valid days). Flag if the increase exceeds both 0.5 hours and twice the baseline median absolute deviation. If MAD is zero, the 0.5-hour floor still applies. These are tunable product heuristics, not clinical thresholds or a significance test. Label weekday mix and coverage changes; collapse overlapping alerts.

03

Lock the contrast before looking.

Classify every eligible note with the same frozen questions, not just notes on high days. Freeze a small enabled subset of the updated context vocabulary before the pilot; medication reported-use capture is separately opt-in and does not automatically enter outcome comparisons. Compare confirmed present versus explicitly absent; report unknown/conflicting counts separately. Pre-register daily stress with same-day context and previous-day context (lag 1). Never search dozens of lags and display only the winner. Same-day timing cannot establish temporal direction.

04

Estimate cautiously and show the denominator.

For lag k, compare stress on day d with exposure on calendar day d−k. Missing days do not shift the alignment. Report group means, their difference, counts, window, and known-context coverage. Require at least 10 valid observations per group to display an exploratory contrast and at least 8 calendar weeks before a 7-day moving-block bootstrap interval (2,000 seeded resamples). These minimums are guardrails, not a power guarantee. Resample complete date-indexed rows, retaining gaps and joint variables; rebuild lag pairs inside sampled blocks, not across artificial seams. Test sensitivity to 3- and 14-day blocks and warn when resamples lack either group.

05

Make competing explanations inspectable.

Before outcomes are viewed, identify weekday, calendar trend, travel, and pre-exposure sleep as candidate confounders. With overlap and adequate history, use a parsimonious regression for the same contrast and block-bootstrap the whole fit; compare adjusted and unadjusted estimates. Do not automatically adjust for same-night sleep if it may mediate evening work. If strata are sparse or exposures inseparable, suppress adjusted estimates and say why. Show all pre-registered comparisons; for formal screening control their false-discovery rate, and label exploratory results. Confirmation belongs in a future untouched period.

06

Do not hide classification error.

Start with user-confirmed context for evidence. Compare results using confirmed-only versus all high-confidence proposals as a labeled sensitivity view, retaining the coverage change. A bootstrap of fixed labels does not include labeling error. Do not multiply Jev confidence by a statistical confidence level or claim calibrated probabilities until held-out human labels support them.

TypeSafe’s autoresearch cookbook shows typed text features feeding a separate CatBoost regressor, with a generative proposer iterating on development errors. It reports wine-review RMSE of 1.87 → 1.77, on 800 held-out reviews, using Jev 1.12—not health validation or proof for Jev 1.13. Its proposer sees outcome labels; that is explicitly not our pilot. 06 / RECOMMENDATION

Prove the labeling value.
Then earn the analytics.

Approve a small, consented evaluation before building a model-powered product. The first success metric is fewer incorrect or burdensome context entries—not an impressive correlation.

PHASE 0 / NOW

Offline contract

100 hand-authored cases covering negation, missing context, conflicts, future plans, quoted text, midnight, travel, decaf, and prompt injection. Test rules and human agreement; this report makes zero Jev calls.

PHASE 1 / WITH CONSENT

Shadow classifier

At most 200 consented notes, five fixed exposure questions, evidence-span selection, no automated publication. Two reviewers label independently without outcomes; adjudicate disagreements. Synthetic cases remain a separate challenge set.

PHASE 2 / ONLY IF USEFUL

Prospective journal

14 days to test editing, clarification burden, and adherence logging. Historical association cards stay gated until enough complete, reviewed history exists. No causal or wellness efficacy claim.

A validation plan that can say “no.”

COST BOUNDARY

Cheap tokens.
Not cheap mistakes.

Current published price: $0.042 per million input tokens; output free. At an assumed 2,000 total input tokens per note including questions, 200 notes cost $0.0168 per pass. Three passes: $0.0504. This is arithmetic, not measured usage.

Proposed hard cap: 1M billable input tokens / $0.042 in model charges, including retries. Stop before exceeding the reserved request budget. Cache by source + rubric + model; never put raw text into logs. Worker/storage costs, plan minimums, and reviewer time are separate. Actual usage.input_tokens determines spend.

PRIVACY BOUNDARY

Minimum text.
Explicit permission.

Cloudflare lists typesafe/jev as third-party with zero data retention. TypeSafe’s direct-service docs separately offer enterprise ZDR. Do not generalize one route’s terms to the other or treat ZDR as “data never leaves.”

Before real inference: review the selected provider’s terms and health-data suitability, get Ron’s opt-in, preview the outgoing excerpt, remove names/places where feasible, send no ring metrics or IDs, and confirm logging/retention controls. Proposed pilot retention: delete inference caches after 30 days; allow immediate source + derivative deletion. No medical-compliance certification is assumed.

Verified Jev constraints · current docs versus proposal
  • Version: TypeSafe currently lists jev-1.13.0; jev-latest and preview resolve to it today. Prefer a pinned direct model; Cloudflare’s route is typesafe/jev. Verify pinning support on that route rather than assuming it. Log the response version and revalidate on change.
  • Context: Cloudflare lists 32,000 tokens. TypeSafe documents 64k total state + all questions, with a 32k state + longest-question sublimit. Use small per-note requests; provider limits are not interchangeable.
  • Input and customization: text / JSON, no image/audio/video input, no customer fine-tuning or LoRA. Request criteria are customization, not training.
  • Known jaggedness: numeric precision, date ordering, indirection, distracting long context, literal wording, adversarial state, and contradictory questions. No free-text generation. Enforce cross-question invariants in code.
  • Confidence: derived from probabilities for Choice/Score; it is not an independent truth verifier. Noul has no separate confidence. Provider claims of calibration still require testing on this domain.

Budget consequence: the illustrative three-pass total exceeds the proposed 1M-token cap. Under these assumptions, run at most two full passes and reserve the remaining 200k tokens for retries or stop early; a third full pass needs a newly approved budget.

THE DECISION FOR RON

Start with Smart Context.
Keep the detective skeptical.

Approve the vocabulary and review experience first. Only then decide whether a consented shadow evaluation is worth running. Do not enable health-data inference, autonomous hypothesis discovery, or production writes as part of this design study.
